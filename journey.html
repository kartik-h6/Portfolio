    Founder & Builder — TechStudyHubCore

    Solo-built learning ecosystem · built alongside B.Pharm

    • Built, alone, a learning ecosystem for pharmacy, nursing and school-level students: a root gateway, a structured B.Pharm architecture, SSLC content, a CGPA calculator on the official PCI 2026 syllabus, and a career navigator.
