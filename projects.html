Telepharmacy · Deployed

PMAS — Pharmacist-led Medication & Adherence Support

An independent telepharmacy build, not attached to any institution: an offline-first, five-language (EN/TE/KN/TA/HI) patient PWA, a pharmacist portal and a FastAPI backend. The privacy model separates identifiable patient records from de-identified clinical data by design, so the two are never stored together.

The counselling text is written by a pharmacist in plain language rather than machine-translated — the point being that translating "twice daily after food" into Telugu changes the language but not the comprehension.

Status: deployed and working as a demo. Not in clinical use, and no patient data has been collected.

EdTech · Archived

TechStudyHubCore

An independent, solo-built learning ecosystem for pharmacy, nursing and school-level students, built on one idea: information is not the same as understanding. It grew into a root gateway, a structured B.Pharm learning architecture, SSLC content, a CGPA calculator on the official PCI 2026 syllabus, and a career navigator.

It is now being retired. As one person alongside M.Pharm and PMAS, the ecosystem became too broad to maintain responsibly — a team was hoped for but never formed. Rather than let it rot, the engineering record has been archived so the reasoning survives the domain.

Research · B.Pharm final-year thesis

Telepharmacy — comparative literature review

My final-year dissertation, carried out individually from start to finish: literature search and screening, evidence synthesis, writing and presentation. It compared remote and face-to-face pharmacy counselling.

Satisfaction came out high in both models — but trust and emotional reassurance still leaned toward in-person care. That gap is what made the project interesting: it reframes the question from which model wins to which mode suits this patient at this point in the journey.
