RGUHS dissertation · 2025

Patient satisfaction & counselling effectiveness in telepharmacy

A comparative literature review of remote versus face-to-face pharmaceutical care.

The review compared remote and in-person counselling across patient satisfaction, trust and communication. Satisfaction came out high in both models — but trust and emotional reassurance still leaned toward in-person care. That gap reframed the question entirely.

Rather than treating telepharmacy and face-to-face care as competing models, the work argues the useful question is mode-appropriateness — and that healthcare communication is not simply information transfer: it carries tone, pauses, empathy and non-verbal cues that technology cannot fully replace.
