The problem

Two failures I keep running into

Both are communication failures, not knowledge failures — and both are fixable.

01 · Capability

The Capability Gap

Health systems generate enormous volumes of evidence-based material. It arrives as information overload rather than understanding — technically correct, practically unusable.

02 · Comprehension

The Comprehension Gap

At the point of care, patients need immediate, simple and culturally synchronised guidance to stay adherent and manage chronic conditions. That layer is usually missing.

hypothesis
technology-assisted, vernacular, visual-first communication measurably improves adherence
outcome
fewer avoidable re-hospitalisations
method
evidence synthesis → plain-language design → localised delivery
